The **RRGC** (Rapid Response Global Cavalry) is a joint military and civilian outfit. It combines top competitive-sport talent with fast tactical response and search-and-rescue capability, and it has informal support ties to the [17th IEG](17th-ieg.md).

Its scouts run the **Twin Spear Doctrine**: Vantor scouts and secures the route, and Solance follows with stabilization and extraction. Together they're called the fastest deployable crawler pair on the planet.

The Cavalry doesn't have a crest, motto, colours or nickname yet.
