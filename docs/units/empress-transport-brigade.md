The **Empress Transport Brigade** (ETB) is the civilian logistics arm of the "Stargate" program (an informal name that isn't final). Its high-clearance, high-prestige civilian contractors run heavy-haul Scania rigs alongside the military, but not under military command.

Its culture is immaculate rigs and elite drivers: the NASA contractors of this universe, but with Scania V8s and attitude.

## Divisions

- Heavy Civilian Haul Division
- Rapid Response Logistics Cell
- Civilian Gate Transit Support

## Known members

- [Pug](../characters/pug.md) (Unit 1501G1), lead hauler
- [Hangman](../characters/hangman.md) (1572CR1), crane

Its military counterpart is the [3rd Expeditionary Transport Battalion "Iron Wolves"](3etb-iron-wolves.md).
