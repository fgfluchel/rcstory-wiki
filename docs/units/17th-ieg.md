The **17th IEG** (17th Interspatial Engineering Group, "The Bedrock Division") is a small, elite, multi-disciplinary Tier-1 covert unit nested inside [CIOD](ciod.md). Think of it as the special forces of engineering: interdimensional construction, humanitarian extraction, recon and stabilization. Its motto is **"We Build Beyond."**

Its core jobs are stabilizing [rift zones](../phenomena/rift-zones.md), building portal foundations, and supporting [RRGC](rrgc.md) deployments.

## Symbol

A three-layer foundation block split by a vertical rift line.

## Stranded in the slices

After the 17th IEG was stranded in an unknown multiverse, it found it could not navigate back to Earth or its home dimension. For reasons nobody yet understands, it *can* reliably return to [Slice 10904](../locations/slice-10904.md). So the unit built a permanent base there: a research hub, a staging area for expeditions, and the seed of a future civilization.
