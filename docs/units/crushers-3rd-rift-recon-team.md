The **Crushers 3rd Rift Recon Team** is the formal name of the team known as **Rocky Recon**. It handles heavy breach entry, rescue and extraction, and hazard-zone terrain operations.

It is a different team from the [Rocky Recon Interspatial Squad](rocky-recon-interspatial-squad.md), despite the similar names.

## Roster

- [Hulk](../characters/hulk.md), the Green Crusher — heavy breach entry
- [Ole Red](../characters/ole-red.md) — rescue and extraction
- [Yellow Crusher](../characters/yellow-crusher.md) — hazard-zone operations
