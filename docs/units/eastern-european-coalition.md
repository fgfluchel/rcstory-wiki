The **Eastern European Coalition** (EEC) is a joint armored division formed after the **Wendaska Accords**. It represents a unified Eastern Front alliance under one banner and specializes in heavy transport, missile systems and cold-region logistics. Its motto is **"Steel Unites the East."**

Its primary base is Sector V-17, in [The Hive](../locations/the-hive.md) Sub-Complex. Its vehicles include the WPL C8815 tractor.
