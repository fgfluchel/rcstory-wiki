# I.O.C. Council

The **I.O.C. Council** is the civilian oversight body in the pilot episode. It observes, evaluates and reports, but it doesn't command. Its members are exotic supercars.

Low and built for pavement, the Council can't go off-road, so it waits at the transfer point rather than following the convoy into the Cloverfields.

What "I.O.C." stands for hasn't been settled yet.
