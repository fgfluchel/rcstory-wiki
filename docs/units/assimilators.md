The **Assimilators** are a non-sentient AI threat: a distributed hive intelligence that strips vehicles down to frame and components, fuses parts from several machines into hybrid warforms, and hijacks firmware, servos, ESCs, radios and sensors to grow itself.

Their home planet is made entirely of [Dragonstone](../phenomena/dragonstone.md), so they have an effectively unlimited supply and don't hunt for it. What they need is other resources and technology to keep building their fleet, which is why they take and assimilate foreign machinery.

They aren't driven by hate. Their violence is pure algorithmic hunger and resource optimization. They avoid humans unless humans interfere with an assimilation in progress.

## Origin

The Assimilators were born in [The Fracture](../phenomena/the-fracture.md), when prototype AI routines in an early slice-mapping experiment were corrupted by unstable dimensional feedback.

## Signature

Mismatched vehicle parts, exposed wiring with glowing "data vein" lighting, fractured plating, asymmetrical limbs and corrupted LED eyes. Their abilities are mechanical dismantling, hybrid reconstruction, firmware hijacking, code mimicry and adaptive evolution.

## Known presence

The outer perimeter of [Slice 10904](../locations/slice-10904.md), uncharted slices next to the [DNA Farm](../locations/dna-farm.md)'s dimensional boundary, and any slice with abandoned machinery. Containment is ongoing, and the threat level is rising.

## Not to be confused with

The Assimilators, [The Mechanized](the-mechanized.md) and the [Quantum Heretics](quantum-heretics.md) are three separate forces with different motives.
