The **3rd Expeditionary Transport Battalion "Iron Wolves"** is the military counterpart to the civilian [Empress Transport Brigade](empress-transport-brigade.md). It provides tactical, armored heavy lift and convoy logistics for the [17th IEG](17th-ieg.md), securing convoys and moving armored units in support of off-world and interdimensional engineering missions.

Its culture is wolf-pack convoy discipline and a protective streak toward engineering teams. It's known for "silent rollouts": engines idling low, lights off.

## Insignia

An angular metallic wolf-head silhouette inside a gate-shaped border, in charcoal with gate-blue accents, lettered "3ETB / IRON WOLVES." The mascot is a steel-plated wolf with glowing gate-blue eyes.
