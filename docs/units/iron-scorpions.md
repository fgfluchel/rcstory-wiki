The **Iron Scorpions** is the nickname of the [17th IEG](17th-ieg.md) field crew caught in the [Shadowrift Barricade Incident](../phenomena/shadowrift-barricade-incident.md). The official mascot is a scorpion, but [K-Tool](../characters/k-tool.md) is the crew's emotional mascot.

- [Björn Stormweld](../characters/bjorn-stormweld.md)
- [Hangman](../characters/hangman.md)
- [Ole Red](../characters/ole-red.md)
- Support vehicles, including an excavator and a Humvee escort
