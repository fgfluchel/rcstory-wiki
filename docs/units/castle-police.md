**Castle Police** (Installation PD) is the lawful bridge between the civilian world and the SPA world. Its Traffic Control Division secures intersections, stops civilian traffic, gives the convoy right of way, and keeps routes clear for major operations such as B-36 redeployments and Scania transfer runs.

It's a separate, official authority. It isn't part of the convoy, the rookies or [K2](../characters/k2.md)'s patrol.

## In the pilot

Castle PD blocks the intersection as the Kickstarter convoy rolls through, while the [I.O.C. Council](ioc-council.md) waits at the transfer point.
