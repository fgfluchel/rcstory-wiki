**RMT-Aegis** is a squad-sized armored recon element nested inside the [17th IEG](17th-ieg.md). It runs light armor, Hummers and scout vehicles for perimeter security, rapid extraction, escort and dimensional-entry support.

It is a field team, separate from the higher [Aegis Command Group](aegis-command-group.md).

## The Rift Arrival Sequence

A planned shot that doubles as an in-universe moment: the fleet driving one at a time out of a leafy wood-line trail, as the 17th IEG materializes from dimensional transit. Stone leads, then Violent and Specter, and the heavy armor (Winfield Actual, Leopard Actual, HOTEL-1) closes it out for visual weight.

The team operates out of [The Hive](../locations/the-hive.md) for Rift monitoring, vehicle staging and SPA node access.
