The **Quantum Heretics** believe they are supreme beings and stand as enemies of every other race. They use methods that aren't fully understood, sometimes not even by themselves, in their efforts to wipe out other civilizations. They're the only one of the three antagonist forces driven by ideology rather than resources.

They are dimensional saboteurs and [Darkstone](../phenomena/darkstone.md) harvesters. They believe Darkstone is a "god-key" to transcending physical form, and they want to destabilize dimensions by controlling it. They placed the barricade in the [Shadowrift Barricade Incident](../phenomena/shadowrift-barricade-incident.md) to trap the 17th IEG and capture any [Gifted](../phenomena/the-gifted.md) among them. It's rumored, but not confirmed in the story, that they can permanently corrupt a Gifted individual through "quantum brainwashing."

"Quantum Heretics" is a working name and may change.

## Not to be confused with

The [Assimilators](assimilators.md), [The Mechanized](the-mechanized.md) and the Quantum Heretics are three separate forces.
