**Aurora 16** is an elite, self-contained special-operations squad of four, able to operate on land, at sea, in the air and in space.

The squad is fresh out of the [DNA Farm](../locations/dna-farm.md) and is going through its first evaluation and observation training at [Slice 10904](../locations/slice-10904.md). It was carried in on [Scania](../characters/scania.md)'s flatbed, and [Halden Cross](../characters/halden-cross.md) manages its deployment windows. The four members haven't been named yet.
