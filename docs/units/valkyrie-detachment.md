The **Valkyrie Detachment** is a fast-deploying airborne detachment of the [17th IEG](17th-ieg.md). It was formed to enter unstable [rift zones](../phenomena/rift-zones.md) before ground teams can mobilize, and it is the first hybrid unit to combine rotor-wing recon with mechanized terrain control. Its motto is **"We Rise Where Others Fall."**

## Composition

- [Valkyrie](../characters/valkyrie.md)'s MD-500 — squad leader and aerial overwatch
- [Aurek](../characters/aurek.md) ("Stonehand") — foundation specialist
- A Huina 1594 excavator — rift breach and trenching
- A Huina 1710 dump hauler
- A Huina 1569 bulldozer — surface reformation

## Symbol and principles

A winged spanner crossing a stabilizer blade, encircled by a faint halo. Its principles: speed over mass, precision over power, honor over glory.
