The **CIOD Field Engineering Corps** is the corps-level engineering force of [CIOD](ciod.md). It is responsible for [TitanForge](../locations/titanforge.md) infrastructure, [Rift](../phenomena/rift-zones.md) stabilization and technical field operations. Its identity is industrial and mechanical, distinct from the tactical identity of Aegis.

## Crest

A gear-based emblem around a central lightning bolt or power node, in industrial yellow with steel grey, charcoal and silver accents.
