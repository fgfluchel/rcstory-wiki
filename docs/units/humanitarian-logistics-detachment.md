The **Humanitarian Logistics Detachment** (HLD), known by its mascot name the **Iron Wolves**, is the [17th IEG](17th-ieg.md)'s military logistics arm and the counterpart to the civilian [Empress Transport Brigade](empress-transport-brigade.md). It provides armored heavy lift and convoy logistics, securing convoys and moving armored units and humanitarian cargo in support of off-world and interdimensional engineering missions.

Its culture is wolf-pack convoy discipline and a protective streak toward engineering teams. It's known for "silent rollouts": engines idling low, lights off.

Older drafts also call it the 3rd Expeditionary Transport Battalion (3ETB).

## Insignia

An angular metallic wolf-head silhouette inside a gate-shaped border, in charcoal with gate-blue accents, lettered "IRON WOLVES." The mascot is a steel-plated wolf with glowing gate-blue eyes.
