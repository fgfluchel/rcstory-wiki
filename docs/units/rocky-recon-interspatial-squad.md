The **Rocky Recon Interspatial Squad** is a light recon unit built around FJ Cruiser-class crawlers. It is a different team from the [Crushers 3rd Rift Recon Team](crushers-3rd-rift-recon-team.md) ("Rocky Recon"), despite the similar names.

## Roster

- FJ Yellow — primary point scout (see [minor characters](../characters/minor-characters.md))
- FJ Blue — recon overwatch. This is [Vantor](../characters/vantor-and-solance.md), who also serves with the [RRGC](rrgc.md).
