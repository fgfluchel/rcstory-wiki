**The Veilwardens** are the oldest secret society in the setting. They were the first to sense [the Shadowrift](../phenomena/rift-zones.md) beneath [Wendaska](../locations/wendaska.md), long before modern nations existed, and they guarded it for generations.

When the anomaly began to stir, the Veilwardens warned world leaders. That warning led to the formation of the Shadowrift Battalion, whose descendants are tied to [SPA](../locations/the-spa.md) operations.

## The Veil Sigil

A thin vertical line splitting a circle: the boundary between worlds. It's deliberately simple, small enough to sit quietly on a license plate or a badge.
