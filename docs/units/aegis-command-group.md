The **Aegis Command Group** is the central command hub for every Aegis-level mission, scout team, engineering group and [Rift-zone](../phenomena/rift-zones.md) operation. It is the operational heart of Aegis, bridging the [RMT Strategic Council](rmt-strategic-council.md)'s oversight with the units in the field. Its motto is **"Strength Through Coordination."**

Don't confuse it with [RMT-Aegis](rmt-aegis.md), the armored recon team inside the 17th IEG. The Command Group is the higher command hub; RMT-Aegis is a field team.

## Crest

A shield with chevrons or wings around a central command-nexus node, in gunmetal grey with crimson red and black-and-silver accents.
