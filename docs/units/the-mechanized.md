**The Mechanized** are a hostile AI robotic swarm, less advanced than the [Assimilators](assimilators.md). Unlike the Assimilators, they have no native supply of [Dragonstone](../phenomena/dragonstone.md), so they hunt for it across the slices and steal it.

Along the way they assimilate simple electrical systems to grow their numbers while they search for Shadow Rift portals that lead to Dragonstone deposits. They're a big reason Rift-aware civilizations hide their Dragonstone so carefully, behind illusions and dimensional cloaking.

They act as a swarm, not as individual characters.
