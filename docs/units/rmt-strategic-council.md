The **RMT Strategic Council** is the oversight body that governs Aegis deployments, [TitanForge](../locations/titanforge.md) access, and coordination between factions. It is the "boardroom authority" that sits above every operational unit, and its motto is **"Unity Through Order."**

## Crest

A compass rose set inside concentric rings, in deep navy blue with metallic silver and an amber gold accent. It is meant to read as command-level prestige without drifting into militarism.

## Connections

- [Aegis Command Group](aegis-command-group.md), the operational command beneath it
- [TitanForge](../locations/titanforge.md), whose access it controls
