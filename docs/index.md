# RC Story Wiki

Welcome to the official lore wiki of the **RC Story Cinematic Universe (RCSU)**, produced by House Fluchel Studios.

In the RCSU, real RC machines become characters. Loaders, dump trucks, crawlers and heavy haulers carry callsigns, belong to units, and work the tunnels of **Mount Titan**. Each rig here is a real RC vehicle, torn down and tested in the Mechatronics Bay before it goes on camera.
