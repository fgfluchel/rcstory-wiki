# Mount Titan

**Mount Titan** is the anchor of the RC Story Cinematic Universe. It is one unified geography that contains [TitanForge](titanforge.md) and [The Hive](the-hive.md), and it is entered through [The SPA](the-spa.md).

## In the real world

Mount Titan is planned as a physical set: a volcano roughly 20 × 20 × 10 ft, built for filming the series.
