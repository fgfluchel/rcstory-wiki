**Slice 10904** is the home slice: one dimension among an infinite, mostly undiscovered number of slices. It contains [The Castle](the-castle.md), [The SPA](the-spa.md) and the [DNA Farm](dna-farm.md). The [Base Director](../characters/base-director.md)'s authority covers this slice only.

## Why it became home

After the [17th IEG](../units/17th-ieg.md) was stranded in an unknown multiverse, it discovered it couldn't navigate back to Earth or its home dimension, but it could reliably return to this slice, for reasons not yet understood. So it built a permanent base here: a research hub, a staging area for expeditions, and the seed of a future civilization.

## Dangers and treasures

Hostile mechanical entities occasionally cross over from [the Rift](../phenomena/rift-zones.md), which is why [Ronin Dex](../characters/ronin-dex.md) is assigned here. [Assimilators](../units/assimilators.md) have been seen at the slice's outer perimeter. The slice's physics-reinforced boulders hold [Dragonstone](../phenomena/dragonstone.md).

The slice is also home to giant cats, which the 17th IEG crew protects and supports as a humanitarian mission.
