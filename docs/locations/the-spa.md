**The SPA** is the hidden entrance to [Mount Titan](mount-titan.md). It's a deliberately unremarkable, windowless "spa" building on a lonely road in [Wendaska](wendaska.md), nondescript on purpose so nobody looks twice. Only a handful of people on Earth know it covers a deep, fortified access point to the Shadowrift.

Out front there's a small, mundane waiting room with a bored attendant, a vague "spa services" menu, and a door marked "Employees Only." Behind that door is a hidden, servo-driven roll-up garage door disguised under faux log siding, which opens onto the tunnel into the mountain.

In-universe it's played straight. Characters talk about "meeting at the SPA," "taking the SPA entrance" and "routing the convoy through the SPA node" as completely normal operational language.

## The access tunnel

Behind the garage door is a large-diameter corrugated tunnel, staged as a cinematic convoy entrance: vehicles drive through one after another. Each vehicle is filmed once, so the entrance and exit shots can be reused across many episodes.
