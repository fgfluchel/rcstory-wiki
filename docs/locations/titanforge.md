**TitanForge** is an immense cave and tunnel network within [Mount Titan](mount-titan.md). Access is governed by the [RMT Strategic Council](../units/rmt-strategic-council.md), and its infrastructure is maintained by the [CIOD Field Engineering Corps](../units/ciod-field-engineering-corps.md).

It has an Ops Bay, a white-room quantum matter scan that every entrant must pass, and a tiered classification system ([Björn](../characters/bjorn-stormweld.md)'s file cites "TitanForge Protocol Level 7").

## The path through TitanForge

Rookies arrive from the [DNA Farm](dna-farm.md) with no idea that the Shadowrift or other slices exist. They first have to pass the [Mulch-Bed Evaluation](../phenomena/mulch-bed-evaluation.md) at [Wendaska](wendaska.md). Only then do they move through TitanForge to its Ops Bay, where they receive accessory upgrades and become [Rift Qualified](../phenomena/rift-qualified.md).

Hydraulic bodies are a separate, much later arc. See [Hydraulic Evolution](../phenomena/hydraulic-evolution.md).

## The name

TitanForge got its name when Björn Stormweld first arrived at The SPA. Driving through the huge tunnel beyond the entrance, Björn, rumored to descend from Nordic gods, was awed by its scale and wondered whether gods had built it. The name stuck on the spot, along with the caption: *"Even Björn pauses. TitanForge does that to gods."* A TitanForge theme song came out around the same time.
