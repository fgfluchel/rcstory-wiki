**Wendaska** is a small, forgotten town where [The SPA](the-spa.md) sits. In the story, early tribes called it "The Dark Opening" because they sensed the Shadowrift beneath it long before anyone understood what it was. (That meaning is invented for the story; it isn't a real-world translation.)

Wendaska is also the proving ground inside [Slice 10904](slice-10904.md) where fresh [DNA Farm](dna-farm.md) units face their first sorting, the [Mulch-Bed Evaluation](../phenomena/mulch-bed-evaluation.md).

## Connections

- [The Veilwardens](../units/veilwardens.md), the first guardians of the Shadowrift beneath the town
- [The Hive](the-hive.md), beneath Wendaska
- The Wendaska Accords, which formed the [Eastern European Coalition](../units/eastern-european-coalition.md)
