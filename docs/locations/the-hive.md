**The Hive** is a subterranean operations center inside [Mount Titan](mount-titan.md), beneath [Wendaska](wendaska.md). [RMT-Aegis](../units/rmt-aegis.md) uses it for Rift monitoring, vehicle staging, engineering and SPA node access. Sector V-17, The Hive Sub-Complex, is the primary base of the [Eastern European Coalition](../units/eastern-european-coalition.md).

## In the real world

The Hive is the series' underground filming studio. It started with mining-style lighting, and it's planned as a three-chamber soundstage: a main chamber for the city core and hero set, an uphill-sloped wing, and a smaller opposite wing. The miniature city is built from repurposed 12-inch wire-cube storage panels, used as 1/14-scale building frames.
