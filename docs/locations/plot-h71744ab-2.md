**Plot H71744AB-2** is a hostile dimension next to a warzone. The [17th IEG](../units/17th-ieg.md) arrived there by accident, after a Shadowrift misdial caused by a [Darkstone](../phenomena/darkstone.md) flare, while on what was meant to be a humanitarian mission: building an irrigation creek for a friendly ally.

It holds a significant Darkstone concentration, and it's where the [Shadowrift Barricade Incident](../phenomena/shadowrift-barricade-incident.md) happens. A future mission, **Operation Dark Retrieval**, is planned: a fully armored return to secure the Darkstone before the [Quantum Heretics](../units/quantum-heretics.md) can weaponize it, and to find out who placed the barricade.
