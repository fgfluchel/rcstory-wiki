The **DNA Farm** is where new machines are printed and born before they enter [Project H](../phenomena/project-h.md). It exists to develop crews and hardware that can survive [Slice 10904](slice-10904.md)'s reinforced physics long enough to extract [Dragonstone](../phenomena/dragonstone.md) safely.

## Genesis numbers

Every unit that rolls out of the Farm is issued a numeric decal from 0 to 9, its **genesis number**. It's a birth and identity code, not a name, callsign or rank. [Mûmak](../characters/mumak.md) carries #1, marking him as the first to awaken and the first hero of the Sorting.

## After the Farm

Fresh units are carried to [Wendaska](wendaska.md) for the [Mulch-Bed Evaluation](../phenomena/mulch-bed-evaluation.md). Those who don't show promise are "recycled back into the Farm."
