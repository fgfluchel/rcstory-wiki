**The Rift** is the dimensional anomaly at the center of the universe and the reason [RMT-Aegis](../units/rmt-aegis.md) and the [17th IEG](../units/17th-ieg.md) exist. It isn't a planet-to-planet gate. It's dimensional, state-shifting travel: cross-reality resonance and identity-anchored crossings between slices.

"The Rift" is the plain, working name. **"Shadowrift"** is the myth and legend built around it, used for anything musical or legendary, and it already has its own song, "Shadowrift Horizons."

**Rift zones** are the unstable areas around it. They call for [Aegis Command Group](../units/aegis-command-group.md) operations and stabilization by the [CIOD Field Engineering Corps](../units/ciod-field-engineering-corps.md), and the [Valkyrie Detachment](../units/valkyrie-detachment.md) exists to get into them before ground teams can.

## What powers it

[Dragonstone](dragonstone.md) powers and stabilizes the Rift. Without it, the Rift would collapse and the slices would drift apart. [Darkstone](darkstone.md) acts as a dialing amplifier, and [the Gifted](the-gifted.md) can choose where they cross to.

## Crossing on camera

- **Chromatic Transit Array.** The original idea for filming a Rift crossing: a rainbow tunnel lit with strobes and fog. Each colour ring is a dimensional wavelength, the strobes are a hull-sync effect, and the fog is condensing Rift plasma.
- **Rift Arrival Sequence.** Vehicles emerging one at a time from a wood-line trail. See [RMT-Aegis](../units/rmt-aegis.md).

## Rift Purge

A running joke: everyday chores get narrated as "dimensional stabilization" rituals, because the Rift always leaves residue, and someone has to clean it.
