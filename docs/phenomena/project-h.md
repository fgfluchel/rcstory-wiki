**Project H**, the Hyper-Nordic Genetic Engineering Initiative, is a program whose participants are known as *candidates*. At least one participant, [Konk](../characters/konk.md), is a **Gifted Variant**.

Project H clones are engineered because they inherit [the Gifted](the-gifted.md) trait: the rare ability to choose a destination through the Shadowrift.

New machines are born at the [DNA Farm](../locations/dna-farm.md), sorted in the [Mulch-Bed Evaluation](mulch-bed-evaluation.md), and outfitted at [TitanForge](../locations/titanforge.md). Candidates are the characters marked for a later [hydraulic evolution](hydraulic-evolution.md).
