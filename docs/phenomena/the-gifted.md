# The Gifted

**The Gifted** are rare individuals who can actively "dial" a destination through the Shadowrift instead of just passing through it. Project H clones inherit the trait, which is the reason [Project H](project-h.md) exists.

[Darkstone](darkstone.md) amplifies the ability. The Gifted can also sense Darkstone concentrations and detect sabotage of the Rift.

[Konk](../characters/konk.md) is a Gifted Variant. The [Quantum Heretics](../units/quantum-heretics.md) try to capture the Gifted, and they're rumored to be able to corrupt one through "quantum brainwashing."
