**Darkstone** is a quantum-reactive mineral found across many dimensions. It's a dimensional catalyst, a dialing amplifier for Shadowrift travel, a power source and a weapon, which makes it highly sought after. Unlike an inert resource, it's quantum-"alive": it reacts to intention, and it responds specifically to [the Gifted](the-gifted.md).

Darkstone is a different substance from [Dragonstone](dragonstone.md), despite the similar name.

## In the story

- The [Quantum Heretics](../units/quantum-heretics.md) harvest it and see it as a "god-key."
- A Darkstone flare misdialed the 17th IEG into [Plot H71744AB-2](../locations/plot-h71744ab-2.md).
- Darkstone boulders formed the barricade in the [Shadowrift Barricade Incident](shadowrift-barricade-incident.md).
