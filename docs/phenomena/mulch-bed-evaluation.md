The **Mulch-Bed Evaluation** is the first sorting ritual for machines fresh out of the [DNA Farm](../locations/dna-farm.md). It's held at the [Wendaska](../locations/wendaska.md) proving grounds, before any of them know about the Shadowrift, [TitanForge](../locations/titanforge.md) or what [Project H](project-h.md) really is.

Each machine is judged on ground sense, load discipline, articulation control, terrain adaptation, instinct under pressure and behavioral stability. Those who show promise move on toward TitanForge. Those who don't are recycled back into the Farm.

It's quiet, tense, scientific and ceremonial, not a mission and not a fight.
