**Hydraulic Evolution** is the long-term arc in which [Project H](project-h.md) candidates evolve into hydraulic forms. When one does, the rest of the cast reacts with awe at the upgraded machine's presence.

It's a separate, later arc from the [TitanForge](../locations/titanforge.md) accessory upgrades. Evolution comes a few seasons down the road, not right after a rookie passes evaluation.

## In the real world

Every character vehicle can be bought in real life. As each candidate's real hydraulic version is bought, that character evolves in the story. So far only one hydraulic vehicle exists: [Earthshaker](../characters/earthshaker.md), [K-Tool](../characters/k-tool.md)'s evolved form.
