The **Shadowrift Barricade Incident** is a flash-forward, set a year ahead of the main story.

On a humanitarian mission in [Plot H71744AB-2](../locations/plot-h71744ab-2.md), a crew from the [17th IEG](../units/17th-ieg.md), the Iron Scorpions, finds its Shadowrift exit deliberately blocked by strange boulders, later identified as [Darkstone](darkstone.md).

1. [K-Tool](../characters/k-tool.md), the rookie dozer, tries first and fails. The veterans don't laugh; they admire the effort.
2. [Aurek](../characters/aurek.md) tries next.
3. [Hangman](../characters/hangman.md) steps in with heavy lifting.
4. The crew falls back to the Castle.

It's the moment the crew realizes something is working against them on purpose, though they don't yet know who or why. (It was the [Quantum Heretics](../units/quantum-heretics.md).)

## On the convoy

Aurek, K-Tool, [Björn Stormweld](../characters/bjorn-stormweld.md)'s dump truck, Hangman, [Huxley](../characters/huxley.md) as the precision digger, a Humvee escort and [Ole Red](../characters/ole-red.md).

## What comes next

Operation Dark Retrieval, a fully armored return to secure the Darkstone and find out who placed the barricade.
