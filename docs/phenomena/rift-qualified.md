**Rift Qualified** is the status a vehicle earns once its [TitanForge](../locations/titanforge.md) accessory outfitting is complete. Vehicles arrive "bare bones," stock and straight out of the box. They only become Rift Qualified after they're fitted with their detail parts, such as mirrors and hubcaps.

A vehicle running with its lights on before qualification is in an "activation" state, not tactical readiness. It wouldn't run lights in a real tactical situation.

The idea is modeled on jump qualification in the U.S. Army Airborne.
