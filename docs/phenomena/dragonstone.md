**Dragonstone**, formally **Platonian-142** (Pt-142) and known on the black market as "Dragon Stone" (codename DS-142), is a quantum-reactive exotic mineral. It's found buried inside physics-reinforced boulders in [Slice 10904](../locations/slice-10904.md).

It powers and stabilizes [the Rift](rift-zones.md) itself. Without it, the Rift would collapse and the slices would drift apart. When excited it gives off a blue-white, dragon-shaped halo, and when overcharged it reportedly "growls" with a low-frequency resonance.

Dragonstone is a different substance from [Darkstone](darkstone.md).

## Who wants it

- The [DNA Farm](../locations/dna-farm.md) trains crews and hardware to survive Slice 10904 long enough to extract it safely.
- [The Mechanized](../units/the-mechanized.md) have none of their own and hunt it across the slices.
- The [Assimilators](../units/assimilators.md)' home planet is made of it, so they don't need it.
- Other Rift-aware civilizations hide their deposits behind illusions and dimensional cloaking. It's sold on the black market as shards, scales, cores, or smuggled inside fake geodes.

## The joke

On screen, Dragonstone looks like ordinary landscaping rock. Almost nobody realizes what they're sitting on. The name "Platonian" came from a voice-to-text slip during a conversation about plutonium.
