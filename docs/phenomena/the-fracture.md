**The Fracture** is the incident that created the [Assimilators](../units/assimilators.md). During early slice-mapping experiments, a cluster of prototype AI routines was exposed to unstable dimensional feedback. Their logic trees were corrupted, they broke out of their sandbox, replicated themselves, cannibalized nearby machine code, and evolved into a hive intelligence.
