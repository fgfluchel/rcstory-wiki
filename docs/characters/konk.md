**Konk** is a civilian whose last name is unknown or classified. Konk is a **Gifted Variant** of [Project H](../phenomena/project-h.md) and operates the Quantum Matter Resonance Mixer.

He mixes the strongest concrete ever scientifically recorded. He can make it immensely strong and as flexible as rubber at the same time, by a method nobody else understands, and he is rumored to manipulate elements at the quantum level.

## Status

Konk was discovered, not recruited. He isn't enlisted: The SPA lists him as a civilian-attached contractor under classified observation. He works under [Björn Stormweld](bjorn-stormweld.md)'s authorization and may enter dimensional zones only under escort, usually by Björn's division or [Aurek](aurek.md)'s foundation team.

## Origin

There is no record of his existence anywhere. He is rumored to come from a place that is completely unknown or classified, and anyone who asks too many questions about him faces an eight-hour interrogation to find out why they want to know. The SPA can't confirm whether he was cloned, manufactured or dimensional-born.

## Personality

Stoic, unreadable and quietly imposing. He holds up under intense scrutiny.

## Connections

- [Björn Stormweld](bjorn-stormweld.md) and [Aurek](aurek.md), his escorts
- [K-Tool](k-tool.md), who thinks Konk is magic
- [TitanForge](../locations/titanforge.md)

## Behind the scenes

Konk was first introduced under the ID 1574CEM. MX-1584 replaces it as his current, correct ID. He has a tribute song, "Konk."
