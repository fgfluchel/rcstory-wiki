**Sleipnir** is a fast scout and messenger built for extreme terrain, named for Odin's eight-legged horse.

## Two forms

Sleipnir is a shapeshifter with boundaries. He has exactly two forms, and he must commit to one before a mission begins. There is no switching mid-operation.

- **Whisper-Form:** quiet, nimble and scout-class. Moves like wind over stone, nearly traceless, tuned for recon and message-running.
- **Thunder-Form:** big, loud and fast. Full power, full size and full presence, for when speed or intimidation is required.

The shift is literal in the real fleet: the same FMS Bronco exists in 1:18 scale (Whisper-Form) and 1:7 scale (Thunder-Form). Both are one character.

## Personality

Disciplined, purposeful and mission-driven. He doesn't shift on impulse. He weighs the terrain and the stakes before committing to a form.
