**Grom**'s only line, ever, is **"Send it."** The tone scales to the situation: calm and gravelly before a jump, a full-throttle scream in mid-air.

He is the fleet's chaos gremlin, built for cinematic bashing and comedy, and a deliberate tonal outlier next to the more serious military and engineering cast. Like the [F1 Grid](f1-grid.md), all of his personality comes through the delivery of one catchphrase.

## Music

Grom is tied to the basher tracks on the *Servo* album, including "Basher Life," "Kiss the Roof" and "BASH BASH BASH!"
