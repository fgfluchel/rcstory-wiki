**Earthshaker** is a real, working hydraulic RC bulldozer and the future hydraulic-evolution form of [K-Tool](k-tool.md). K-Tool and Earthshaker are the same character in two hardware states: the toy-grade Huina 1554 is the "before," and Earthshaker is the "after."

It is the first, and so far only, hydraulic vehicle in the fleet.

## Hydraulic evolution

Earthshaker is part of the Hydraulic Evolution arc of [Project H](../phenomena/project-h.md), where rookies who prove themselves eventually ascend into full hydraulic machines. K-Tool's evolution is planned for a few seasons down the road.
