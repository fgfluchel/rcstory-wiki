**Director Halden Cross** is Head of Logistics & Transportation for [Project H](../phenomena/project-h.md). He drives the red Gelände II FJ40, Command Unit HX-01.

A former inter-slice convoy commander, he now runs the strategic desk that coordinates every movement in Project H: convoy timing, slice-entry windows, and civilian and military sync. If semi-trucks are involved, Halden is automatically involved.

## Personality

Calm, calculating and unshakeable. He knows every machine by ID, weight and purpose. His job is a command desk, deliberately kept off the front line.
