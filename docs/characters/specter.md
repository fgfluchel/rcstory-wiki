**Specter** (tail designation **Z.E.R.O-3**) is the recon and night-ops element of RMT-Aegis. It works under blackout conditions with an infrared uplink and silent-drive protocols.

Its dark grey finish absorbs Rift-light better than camouflage, which makes it nearly invisible in dimensional haze. A later **Shadow Variant** retrofit adds a winch.

Specter pairs with [Violent](violent.md). See the full roster on [Rowan Hale Abrams](rowan-hale-abrams.md#rmt-aegis-roster).
