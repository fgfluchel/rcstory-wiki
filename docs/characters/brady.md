**Brady** drives a light blue Ford Bronco and wears two hats. He is RMT-Aegis's support and liaison element (Aegis-4), and he is also the civilian perimeter security for [Slice 10904](../locations/slice-10904.md), representing the Founding Organization.

His low-key, friendly, neighborhood-watch energy is a deliberate contrast to the mythic convoy titans like [Mûmak](mumak.md) and [Oliphaunt](oliphaunt.md).

## Personality

Chill and reliable. He doesn't interfere unless he has to, knows the rookies by sight, and probably waves at them. He calls [Wolfslayer](wolfslayer.md) "the ghost truck."

## Connections

- [Halden Cross](halden-cross.md), for logistics movement
- [Base Director](base-director.md), for civilian matters
- [Ronin Dex](ronin-dex.md), his military counterpart in the slice
