**HOTEL-1** is the yellow FMS Hummer that serves as the command vehicle of RMT-Aegis. It carries [Lt. Col. Rowan Hale Abrams](rowan-hale-abrams.md), "Aegis Actual," commander of the armor unit.

HOTEL-1 is the callsign of the vehicle itself, not of a person.

The yellow paint is deliberate. Rowan's philosophy, inherited from Colonel Winfield Lester Abrams, is that "a commander should be seen... so his people know where he is."
