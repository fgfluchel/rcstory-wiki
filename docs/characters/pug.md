**Pug** (Unit **1501G1**) is the lead hauler for the Empress Transport Brigade's civilian heavy-lift operations. He often tows prototype "Stargate" program modules or humanitarian cargo under ETB clearance.

He's Chicago born and bred: loyal, blunt and unflinchingly dependable. He growls through the night shift and never complains.

!!! note "Not Wolfslayer"
    Pug is a separate Scania from [Wolfslayer](wolfslayer.md), the grey Scania 770S.
