**Leopard Actual** was the first non-Abrams platform brought into RMT-Aegis. It is the "scalpel to Winfield's hammer": faster and lighter on its treads, with adaptive targeting optics. Its arrival marked RMT-Aegis expanding beyond domestic command into international coalition territory.

It wears NATO tri-tone camouflage (green, brown and black). [Rowan Hale Abrams](rowan-hale-abrams.md) authorized it after a joint exercise with the European CIOD detachment. It is operated by **Captain Rhea Voss** of the Eastern European Coalition.
