**The Grid** is the RCSU's comedy F1 squad. Each car has one line, and it delivers that line no matter what happens.

| Car | Number & rig | Line | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Leclerc** | #16 Ferrari SF23 | "No!" (escalating to "NOOOOOO!!!!" for real disasters) | The tragic hero with main-character energy. Every situation, good or bad, gets the same devastated reaction. |
| **Verstappen** | #1 Red Bull RB19 (Maisto Tech) | "IDIOT!" | The grid's rage laser. One word with total conviction, aimed at a rival, a teammate, a dropped wrench or a passing cat. |
| **Sainz** | #55 Ferrari SF23 (Sainz livery) | "Let's try something different." | The calm tactician. Meets any chaos, from a spin to a cat sitting on his wing, with the same mild deflection. |
