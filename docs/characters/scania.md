**Scania** is the flatbed carrier that moves rookies, including [Aurora 16](../units/aurora-16.md), from the [DNA Farm](../locations/dna-farm.md) to [Slice 10904](../locations/slice-10904.md) for evaluation. Its operations run under [Halden Cross](halden-cross.md).

Not to be confused with [Wolfslayer](wolfslayer.md) or [Pug](pug.md), who are also Scanias.
