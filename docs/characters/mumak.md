**Mûmak** ("MOO-mock") is the convoy's prime mover. He is the only unit that can haul an articulated dump truck from the [DNA Farm](../locations/dna-farm.md) across both road and off-road terrain without a handoff. He is the off-road counterpart to [Wolfslayer](wolfslayer.md) in endurance and will, not in combat.

He carries a **"#1" genesis decal**, marking him as the first machine to awaken and the first hero of the Sorting. His name comes from the war-elephants of Tolkien's legendarium.

## Personality

Unshakeable stability, absolute duty, silent confidence and zero hesitation. He is protective of the rookies. He knows they don't understand the evaluation, so he carries them gently, like a guardian.

## Real-world basis

The Ural B-36 platform: a 6x6 military-grade logistics truck of about 11 tons, with a V8 diesel of roughly 240 hp.
