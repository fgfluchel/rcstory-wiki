**Winfield Actual** is the heavy armor anchor of RMT-Aegis. The tank is named in tribute to Colonel Winfield Lester Abrams, the mentor behind [Rowan Hale Abrams](rowan-hale-abrams.md)'s family line, which makes it a living memorial in the fleet rather than just hardware.

Rowan touches its hull before every mission.
