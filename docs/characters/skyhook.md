**Skyhook** is the aerial load expert of the [17th IEG](../units/17th-ieg.md), and the Skyhook Aerial Load Team is named after him. As Lift Director he has the final say on rigging any load. If he says a lift isn't safe, it doesn't happen.

He is [Hangman](hangman.md)'s big brother, and the only other person who knows the real story behind Hangman's callsign.

## Personality

Authoritative in the way senior riggers are: calm, exacting, and unwilling to compromise on load safety.
