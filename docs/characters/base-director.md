The **Base Director** is the senior authority inside [Slice 10904](../locations/slice-10904.md), the home dimension. He oversees the Castle, [The SPA](../locations/the-spa.md) and the [DNA Farm](../locations/dna-farm.md), and he handles civilian oversight and Founding Organization directives.

His authority stops at the edge of Slice 10904. He has no say over undiscovered slices or any slice outside 10904.

He has no personal name yet and is known only by his title. He drives the same blue Desert Fox as [Vantor](vantor-and-solance.md).

## Connections

- [Halden Cross](halden-cross.md), who runs logistics and convoy movement
- [Brady](brady.md), who handles civilian security in the slice
