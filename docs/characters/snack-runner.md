The **Snack Runner** is a small, quick pickup with a payload bed. Load the bed with freeze-dried cat treats, drive it across the set, and the cats follow it away from the shoot. It's a live-action distraction based on real cat behavior.

It works alongside [Animal Control Unit 12](animal-control-unit-12.md) in the Cats of Slice 10904 content.
