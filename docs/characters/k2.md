**K2** is the Guardian Entity, the sentinel of the property. He roams the perimeter, watches for disturbances, checks thresholds, oversees Cloverfield transitions, and protects the rookies, the slice and the command center.

## Role

In the pilot episode's command structure K2 is second-in-command. He is stationed inside the hedges at the Materials Center, where he handles load assignments, dispatch, material flow, vehicle staging and rookie coordination, like a tactical commander out of *Small Soldiers*.

## Personality

He is autonomous but respects the Commander's authority, and he still comes to the Commander for the OK.
