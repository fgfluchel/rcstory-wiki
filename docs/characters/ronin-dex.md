**Ronin Dex** (TR-123) is the tactical response unit assigned to [Slice 10904](../locations/slice-10904.md), because the slice is known to host hostile mechanical entities from other dimensions. He works in parallel with [Brady](brady.md) under [Halden Cross](halden-cross.md). Together they stand for military accountability and civilian oversight in the slice.

## Personality

Stoic and precise, and he carries the weight of earlier campaigns. He is autonomous, answers to no single field commander, and only deploys if hostiles breach containment.

## Look

Desert-tan armor, a mounted sensor array and a heavy-caliber deterrent.
