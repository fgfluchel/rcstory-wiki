**Hangman** (ID **1572CR1**) is the crane assigned to the Empress Transport Brigade.

## The callsign

Everyone assumes "Hangman" is a crane joke. It isn't. He was caught with the general's daughter (the general being the head of the entire Engineering Corps) and sentenced to 18 hours suspended from a crane. It didn't scare him off. He married her anyway.

Whenever anyone brings up the "crane theory" in front of his brother [Skyhook](skyhook.md), the two of them break out laughing and never explain why.

## Personality

Stubborn, unshaken by consequences, and quietly romantic under the tough-guy legend. He terrifies [K-Tool](k-tool.md), who tries not to show it.

## The ID

His ID follows the fleet's label-maker nomenclature of model number, type code and unit number: **1572** is the model, **CR** is crane, and **1** marks the first crane, which leaves room for a future 1572CR2.

## Coming up

A flashback episode, *Hangman*, will tell his origin. He also has a tribute song, "Eighteen Hours."
