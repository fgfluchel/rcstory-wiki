**Lieutenant Colonel Rowan Hale Abrams**, callsign **"Aegis Actual,"** commands RMT-Aegis, the Recon Mobility Team of the [17th IEG](../units/17th-ieg.md). He rides into every mission in the yellow command Hummer [HOTEL-1](hotel-1.md).

## Background

Rowan rose through the Corps of Engineers, special-operations selection and mobility cross-training before he was hand-picked to lead RMT-Aegis. He walked into selection carrying a notebook signed by Colonel Winfield Lester Abrams under the words "Forward, Always."

He isn't related to Winfield by blood. The link is a lineage of mentorship: Winfield trained Rowan's father, and Rowan's father trained Rowan.

## Personality

Quiet intensity, a razor-sharp tactical mind and zero ego. Rowan leads from the front seat of HOTEL-1 rather than from behind armor. He treats the tank [Winfield Actual](winfield-actual.md) as a living memorial and touches its hull before every mission, out of respect rather than superstition.

## Chain of command

Space Command → CIOD → [17th IEG](../units/17th-ieg.md) → RMT-Aegis → Aegis Actual
